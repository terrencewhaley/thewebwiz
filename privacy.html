Privacy Policy

Last updated October 3, 2026

The Web Wiz ("we," "us," "our") operates thewebwiz.us. This page explains what information we collect when you visit the site or get in touch with us, how we use it, and who we share it with.

Information we collect

We collect information in three ways:

  • Contact form. When you submit the contact form, we collect your name, email address, company name, budget range, and the message you write. This is sent to and processed by our form provider, Formspree.
  • Scheduling a call. If you book time with us through a "Start a project" link, you're taken to Calendly, where you provide your name, email, and the time you select. That booking is handled directly by Calendly, under its own privacy policy.
  • Site analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, approximate location, device/browser type, and referring site. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. See Google's Privacy Policy for details, or opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Respond to inquiries and quote requests
  • Schedule and prepare for consultation calls
  • Understand how people find and use the site, so we can improve it

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers named above (Formspree, Calendly, Google Analytics) so they can perform the function we use them for — form delivery, scheduling, and analytics, respectively. We don't share your information with anyone else except where required by law.

Cookies

The only cookies set on this site come from Google Analytics. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings at any time; doing so won't affect your ability to use the site or contact us.

Data retention

We keep contact form submissions and related correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and maintain our business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see "Your rights" below).

Your rights

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@thewebwiz.us. If you're a California resident, this includes rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) to know what personal information we've collected and to request its deletion.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect or use information, we'll update this page and change the date at the top.
